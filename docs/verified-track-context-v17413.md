# V17.4.13 — verified archive → degree → FOGD

Menu 7 stores winner summaries in the existing annual result database. Its new condition-download button calls the same local track archive used by Menu 8, in seven-day windows. A window advances only after distinct, in-range pages satisfy the reported total and rows are saved. Interrupted windows retry safely; successful windows are reused. Older year completion markers are revalidated. PDF errors stay explicit and are retried on the next range download.

The degree run reads the condition archive once, alongside local results and calibration. It indexes reports by date and city and shares the index across races/horses. No condition network requests are added to analysis. Historical reference weather is explicitly a daily observation, not a race-time weather reconstruction. Target weather needs a recorded observation strictly before the race and no later than analysis time. Missing dates/times, measurements, or cities remain missing; no last-known or seasonal estimates enter the modern calculation.

References still require city, surface, exact distance and breed/age group; class fallback remains uncertain. With at least three verified condition comparisons and at least 50% reference coverage, a weighted median replaces the unweighted median. Weather similarity uses exponential distances (temperature 14°C, humidity 45 points, pressure 35 hPa, wind 35 km/h), with minimum weight .2. Missing comparison weights equal the average observed weight so missing records gain no advantage. These are conservative reference-selection heuristics, not coefficients fitted to race outcomes. Prediction accuracy improvement has not yet been measured.

Maintenance is scoped to grass/racing sand/synthetic, never training sand. It requires explicit availability evidence: the first successful PDF download is recorded, which proves availability then, not on the historical report's date. Older records without evidence remain excluded. If two eligible surface reports contain dated operation schedules, a bounded .8–1 similarity factor reflects overlap; absent operation text is not rewarded as confirmed absence. There is no fixed maintenance or weather seconds bonus and no invented pace.

Missing condition support adds one second to uncertainty, not the predicted central time. This uncertainty allowance is a model safeguard, not an empirical confidence interval. FOGD displays total references and verified condition comparisons. Its D is derived from the modern personal degree estimate: the legacy history bridge and old common track score cannot overwrite it or promote a horse without personal history. Undated/future local form histories are excluded and prior form histories are sorted newest first.

Model version is DEGREE-HISTORY-CORE-V17.4.13. Calibration captures race-specific verified conditions and filters to the same model version, so older model residuals do not silently train this model. F/O/G/D weights, nine coupon templates and the isolated tenth method are unchanged.

The actual Adana 2026-09-06 PDF revealed blank maintenance labels falsely counted as completed operations by the old parser. Only operation rows with a date and scheduled time are now parsed. Racing sand, training sand, grass and synthetic section boundaries are normalized for Turkish letter case. Target operations scheduled at/after race time or on future dates are excluded. The schedule is reported as documented, not guaranteed executed. Legacy keyword-only PDF records are refreshed on the next manual range download.
